Octopi are fascinating cephalopods known for their intelligence, adaptability, and unique defense mechanisms. They have eight flexible arms, each lined with suckers that can taste and manipulate objects independently. Their soft bodies allow them to squeeze through tiny spaces, and they use jet propulsion to escape predators. Masters of camouflage, octopuses can change color and texture to blend into their surroundings. They also eject ink clouds to obscure attackers and can regrow lost limbs. Found in oceans worldwide, they are solitary creatures that exhibit remarkable problem-solving skills.
