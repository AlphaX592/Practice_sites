Starfish, or sea stars, are marine invertebrates belonging to the class Asteroidea. They typically have five arms, though some species have more. Found in oceans worldwide, they use tube feet for movement and feeding. Starfish can regenerate lost limbs and even entire bodies in some cases. They prey on mollusks, using their unique ability to extend their stomachs outside their bodies to digest food externally.
