Axolotls (Ambystoma mexicanum) are unique salamanders native to Mexico, known for their ability to regenerate limbs, spinal cords, and even parts of their brains. Unlike most amphibians, they retain their juvenile features throughout life, a trait called neoteny. They are critically endangered due to habitat loss and pollution.
