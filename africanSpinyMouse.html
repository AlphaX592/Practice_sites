The African spiny mouse, belonging to the genus Acomys, is a small rodent known for its coarse, spiny fur. Found across Africa and parts of Asia, it has remarkable regenerative abilities, healing wounds rapidly. It is omnivorous, nocturnal, and often sheds its tail as a defense mechanism against predators.
